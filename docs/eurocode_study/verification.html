7. Verification: how good is FORM here?#

Step 7 of the procedure. Every reliability index of the study is a first-order (FORM) value. The limit state function (4.1) contains products of lognormal and Gumbel variables, so its failure surface is curved in standard-normal space — and FORM, which replaces the surface by its tangent plane at the design point, is then optimistic by a small, systematic amount. Lesson 6 — How good is FORM? Verification and the method decision runs SORM (Breitung’s curvature correction [Breitung, 1984]) and importance sampling around the design point (20 000 samples, standard error 0.004 in β) for all 810 situations of the status quo; the table summarises what it finds.

The FORM error depends on which variable dominates the design point: largest (0.05–0.06) when a lognormal variable does — a model uncertainty or a strength — and smallest (0.015) when the Gumbel load \(Q\) does. By load it is 0.05 for wind and snow and 0.025 for the imposed load. SORM removes it entirely: the remaining differences are within the sampling error.

7.1. What it means for the calibration#

The weighted-mean β of the status quo is 3.74 by FORM and about 3.70 by SORM or sampling. Calibrating with SORM instead of FORM moves the optimum by about +0.01 in both factors (γ_G 1.215 → 1.228, γ_Q 1.750 → 1.761, lesson 6) — a shift of the same order as the resolution of the numbers reported in the study, and far below the sensitivity to the modelling assumptions (weights, characteristic values, coefficients of variation; see the sensitivity study of the lab mode). The AHG study reported FORM values, and so does this documentation; the tool offers SORM everywhere at about twice the cost.

The lesson also checks one situation with 200 000 importance samples and 2 million crude Monte-Carlo samples: FORM 3.520, SORM 3.478, sampling 3.476 ± 0.001 — SORM is right, FORM is 0.04 high, which is the same picture as over the whole model.

In the tool

The Verification step of the learning mode and the Verification tab of the lab mode run this comparison for a sample of the situations of any model, in a few seconds, with the same functions.
